⌜ What Saral does for you ⌟

Understand a contract before it binds you.

  • Clarity

    Legalese, decoded

    “Notwithstanding”, “hereinafter”, “jointly and severally” become sentences you can read once and understand. Terms that carry legal weight are kept and explained, never quietly dropped.

  • Your side

    Know what’s on you

    Tell Saral you’re the tenant, the employee or the borrower, and every clause speaks to you: what you must do, what you can’t do, and what the other side owes you.

  • Accuracy

    Every number stays

    Rent, deposits, deadlines, notice periods and percentages are checked against the original. If the plain version loses one, the clause is redone, and flagged if it still falls short.

  • Fine print

    The small words that matter

    “Notwithstanding anything else”, “if it can be fixed”, “shall not”, “prior written consent”: the words that change a clause’s meaning are checked so simpler never means softer.

  • Language

    Hindi and Marathi too

    Read the plain version in Hindi or Marathi, with the English right beside it to compare.

  • Trust

    The original is always there

    Each plain sentence sits next to the clause it came from, with the contract’s own parties and definitions a click away. Nothing is summarised away or skipped without telling you.

!Your data

No sign-up and nothing to install. Your document is used only to make your plain version: its text is sent to an AI service to be simplified, and your results are cleared when the server restarts. Simplified clauses are kept in a cache on this server so repeat runs are faster. Only upload documents you’re allowed to share.
